Our Story

From Yarn to Little Moments of Joy.

KNOOVI began with a single hook, a ball of yarn, and a quiet Sunday spent making something by hand. What started as a slow, personal craft grew into a small studio built on the same idea: good things take time, and the best gifts carry a little of the maker in them.

We work in small batches, choose our colors and textures with care, and pack every order like it's going to someone we love — because it usually is.
